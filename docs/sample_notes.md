# Study notes

## Photosynthesis
Photosynthesis is the process plants use to turn sunlight, water and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen. It happens in the chloroplasts, where chlorophyll absorbs light, mostly red and blue wavelengths. The oxygen released is a by-product of splitting water molecules.

## French Revolution
The French Revolution began in 1789 with the storming of the Bastille. It ended the absolute monarchy and led to the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen. King Louis XVI was executed in 1793, during the period known as the Reign of Terror.

## Python basics
In Python, lists are mutable, so items can be changed after creation, while tuples are immutable. Dictionaries map keys to values and keep insertion order. A virtual environment keeps a project's dependencies separate from other projects.

## Machine learning
Overfitting happens when a model memorizes its training data and performs poorly on new data. Common remedies are collecting more data, regularization and early stopping. A validation set is used to tune hyperparameters before the final test.

## Personal finance
Compound interest means interest is earned on both the original amount and previously earned interest. An emergency fund should cover three to six months of living expenses. Index funds track a market index and usually have lower fees than actively managed funds.

## Human body
The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. Red blood cells carry oxygen using a protein called hemoglobin. The adult human body has 206 bones.

## Networking
HTTP is the protocol browsers use to request web pages. DNS translates domain names into IP addresses. HTTPS adds TLS encryption so traffic cannot be read in transit.

## Astronomy
The Sun is a star at the center of the Solar System. Light from the Sun takes about eight minutes to reach Earth. Jupiter is the largest planet and has a strong magnetic field.
